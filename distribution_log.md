## 💼 Experiment B: LinkedIn Thought Leadership Campaign

### 🎯 Objective
Write high-value posts on LinkedIn addressing specific bottlenecks (checkpoints, tariff classification, FX rates) to build authority.

### 📝 Team Drafts & Posts

#### 1. Zerihun (Team Lead / Product Owner)
> **Topic:** The True Cost of Border Delays
> 
> Did you know cross-border traders in West Africa lose between 3 to 7 hours per trip simply waiting for tariff clearances? 
> 
> The ECOWAS Common External Tariff (CET) structure is designed to harmonize these costs, but finding the exact bands is a nightmare. Essential social goods (Band 0) start at 0% while sensitive commodities protecting local manufacturing (Band 4) go up to 35%. 
> 
> We at Team-Africod built a free, open-source tool to let you query tariffs instantly and calculate optimal routes. Try it for your next shipment and let us know if it saves you time: https://afritrade-agent.streamlit.app/?ref=linkedin
> 
> \#ECOWAS \#CrossBorderTrade \#Logistics \#AfricaTrade

#### 2. Aja Corneli (Developer)
> **Topic:** The FX Transparency Gap at the Border
> 
> Tired of getting conflicting exchange rate quotes when trading across borders? 
> 
> When hauling cargo between Anglophone and Francophone West Africa, currency fluctuations can eat up your margins before you even cross. 
> 
> We integrated live currency conversion and tariff lookup tables into the AfriTrade Agent. It queries official CET tables and live FX databases so you can calculate your duties in CFA Francs, Naira, or Cedi instantly.
> 
> Check out the open-source code and play with the app: https://afritrade-agent.streamlit.app/?ref=linkedin
> 
> \#Fintech \#OpenSource \#Streamlit \#AfricaTrade

#### 3. Patience (User Research & Testing)
> **Topic:** Cargo Routing: Perishables vs. General Freight
> 
> If you are hauling perishable agricultural goods from Kano to Niamey, the fastest route is not always the best. 
> 
> A route with 2 extra border checkpoints can delay your truck by 6 hours, leading to cargo spoilage. Road conditions and checkpoint density are critical trade variables.
> 
> The AfriTrade Agent helps you compare multi-modal routing options, estimating total distance, checkpoints, and delays based on actual transport studies. 
> 
> Give us your feedback on the tool: https://afritrade-agent.streamlit.app/?ref=linkedin
> 
> \#SupplyChain \#Agriculture \#CorridorLogistics
